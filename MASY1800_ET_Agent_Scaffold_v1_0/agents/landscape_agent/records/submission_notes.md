# Submission notes

## Independent professional judgment draft

A mid-sized packaging plant should test an AI maintenance assistant in shadow mode on one line. Its strongest competitor is a searchable library of manuals and alarm codes linked to its work-order system. The main uncertainty is whether the assistant reliably finds the correct source faster for mixed-OEM equipment. Reassess expansion if a matched replay shows at least 20% lower median time to the correct source across 30 representative incidents, no loss of citation accuracy and no unsafe advice in the sample. These are proposed evaluation thresholds. Verify current connector support and costs before procurement.

## Craft Gained draft

Comparing the two plants showed me that a plausible answer and the correct approved source are different outcomes. In packaging, I would measure the time to the correct source.
